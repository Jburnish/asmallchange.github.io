We are grateful to these local restaurants and businesses for helping make Free Lunch for Folks special. We cannot recommend these great restaurants enough!

Lake City, SC

Thank you for regularly donating delicious muffins and pastries for the Free Lunch for Folks program. Your wonderful food brings a little extra joy to our neighbors each week.

Florence, SC 29505
